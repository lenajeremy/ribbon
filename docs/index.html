Know where your day actually goes

Ribbon tracks the apps and websites you use, shows how you spent your day, and blocks distractions while you work. Tracking happens entirely on your Mac, and your data is stored only there.

Then it keeps you on task

Focus sessions block what you don't need, a score tells you how each one went, and you can ask about your time.

Block what you don't need

Each focus session decides which apps and websites you can open. Ribbon hides the rest until it ends.

Focus on a schedule

A session can start by itself, like 9:00 to 12:00 on weekdays. Its rules stay on until it ends, with one emergency pass a week.

Get a score for every session

When a session ends, Ribbon checks what you did against what you said you'd do, and tells you what cost you points.

Ask about your time

Ask things like “Where did my time go today?” and get an answer from your own activity.

Works with the apps you use

Ribbon tracks every app on your Mac. In Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers, it also sees which website you're on, so it can block sites too.

A focus orb that talks back

During a focus session, a small orb sits in the corner of your screen. Drift for more than a minute and it tells you, out loud. You choose how nice it is.

Free and open source

No account and no subscription. The code is on GitHub under the MIT license.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ribbon free?

Yes. Ribbon is free and open source under the MIT license. The AI features run on your ChatGPT plan (Plus or Pro) or your own OpenAI API key, so any AI costs are between you and OpenAI.

What does the AI see?

Ribbon stores your activity on your Mac. When the AI is on, Ribbon sends app names, website names, page titles and totals to OpenAI, plus screenshots during focus sessions that watch your screen. Screenshots are never saved. If you add a Gemini API key for the orb's voice, what the orb says aloud is sent to Google to be turned into speech.

Does it work without the AI?

Yes. Tracking, the timeline, focus sessions, blocking and session scores all work without it. The AI adds smarter sorting into categories, written summaries, answers to your questions, and the orb's screen checks.

Which browsers does it work with?

Ribbon sees which website you're on in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers, so it can track and block sites there. In Firefox, it sees page titles only.

Can I quit Ribbon during a scheduled session?

No. A scheduled session can't be paused or quit, and its rules stay on through breaks. Your emergency pass, one a week, ends it early. If Ribbon is force-quit during a scheduled session, it reopens within a minute.

Can I install it with Homebrew?

Yes: brew install --cask lenajeremy/tap/ribbon. Ribbon needs macOS 14 or later, and works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
