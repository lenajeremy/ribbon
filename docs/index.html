Ribbon GitHub
Free and open source

The AI time tracker for Mac.

Ribbon records the apps and websites you use, shows where your day went, and blocks distractions when you need to focus.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

For macOS 14 and later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

Tracks itself

Every app and website, timed to the second. Nothing to start or stop, and your data stays on your Mac.

Sorted by AI

New apps and sites land in the right category on their own. Make your own categories by describing them.

Focus sessions

Choose the apps and websites a session allows. Everything else is blocked until you're done.

Ask your day

Ask “Where did my afternoon go?” and get answers from your own log, the way you'd ask a chat.

Free, open source, and private.

Everything you track stays on your Mac. Add your own OpenAI API key in Settings to turn on the AI.
